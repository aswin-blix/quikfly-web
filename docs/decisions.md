| 2026-11-18 | **Cancellation policies are per-service settings** (`cancelPolicyBus/Tours/Rent/Group`, text tiers like `"15d:100, 7d:50"`), applied by the API inside the refund transaction; bookings copy `startsAt` from the quote and record `refundAmount`. | 2026-11-18 | **Services and features are switched in Settings (owner only)**: one switch per service (6) with a customer message, and switches for ride scheduling, extra stops, cash for rides, outstation/one-way, self-drive, trip offers and refer-and-earn.
