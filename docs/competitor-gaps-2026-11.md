# QuikFly leads on plumbing, lags on promises

QuikFly's architecture is ahead of its Indian competitors in three structural ways (one real wallet with atomic debits and instant refunds across six services, an API that sets every price, and an audited admin panel that retunes fares in under 30 seconds) but its customer-facing feature set sits at roughly the 2019 market baseline, and in four places the UI promises rules the API never enforces. The cheapest and most urgent work is therefore not a new feature but making the product truthful: holiday packages print refund slabs ("Free cancellation up to 15 days before travel; 50% refund up to 7 days") while `cancelBookingRow` refunds 100% of every booking, the holiday page promises a 25% block payment, a two-hour expert callback and insurance at checkout that do not exist, the car page hard-codes "24×7 roadside assistance", a ₹250 night charge and a 140 km/day cap that nothing meters, and bus "ladies" seats are a random colour with no API check. Against that baseline, competitors moved in 2025–26 toward scheduled and intercity rides (Uber Reserve 30 minutes to 90 days ahead, Uber Intercity on 3,000+ routes), women-first supply and seating (Uber Moto Women, Rapido Bike Pink, MakeMyTrip's adjacent-berth lock), money-backed trust (Zingbus 50% refund if 45+ minutes late, AbhiBus replacement cab in 45 minutes), self-serve flexibility (IntrCity Smart Switch to 2 hours before departure, Zoomcar Manage Booking to 2 hours before start), paid memberships with cashback (Uber One ₹149/month, up to 10% back), and conversational AI assistants (MakeMyTrip Myra 2.0, Yatra DIYA). Regulation now sets a floor too: MVAG 2025 caps surge at 2×, fixes cancellation penalties at 10% up to ₹100, and requires regional-language apps and panic buttons, while the DPDP Rules 2025 make consent notices and a published DPO contact mandatory by about May 2027. Roughly two-thirds of the gap list is buildable at ₹0 on the current Workers + D1 stack because QuikFly already has runtime settings, a generic catalog, an audited admin and a wallet ledger; the rest (real OTP, payments, WhatsApp, maps, KYC, insurance, LLM assistant) is correctly parked behind client sign-off, and this report names the provider and entry price for each.

## Four screens promise rules the API does not enforce

The most damaging gaps are the ones a client can trip over in a demo, and every one of them is a settings-or-pricing change rather than new infrastructure. The research read QuikFly's own code alongside its competitors, and the pattern is consistent: copy was written to look like a market-standard product, but the enforcement layer was never built, which also breaks AGENTS.md Rule 3 (no hard-coded tunables).

| Where | What the customer reads | What the system does | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holidays (`packages/shared/src/catalog.ts` lines 66–115; `apps/api/src/services/bookings.ts` lines 114–137) | Per-package slab text, e.g. "Free cancellation up to 15 days before travel; 50% refund up to 7 days" | `cancelBookingRow` refunds 100% to the wallet on every cancellation; no slab logic exists | Store slabs as structured policy (days-before → % refund) and compute the refund server-side from the booked start date |
| Holidays (`apps/web/src/screens/HolidayScreens.tsx` lines 208–225) | "Pay in full now, or 25% to block and the balance 15 days before travel"; "A travel expert calls within 2 hours of booking"; "Travel insurance (add at checkout)" | Quote takes `{ tourId, adults, kids }` only; no part-payment, no enquiry table, no insurance line | Either build (wallet-based block + `due_at` + enquiry queue) or delete the copy before the next demo |
| Holidays (`packages/shared/src/types.ts` line 337) | A start-date picker on the detail page | The date never reaches the API; peak and off-peak cost the same | Add `start` to the tour quote; later, departures with seats |
| Rent a car (`apps/web/src/screens/RentScreens.tsx`) | "24×7 roadside assistance", "Night charge ₹250 after 10 PM", "Driving licence (1+ year old), Aadhaar or passport, 21+", "Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. Deposit refunded within 48 hours" | None is a setting or a quote line; cancellation is unconditional full refund at any time | Move each to `Settings`; add night-charge and cancellation-tier rules to `pricing.ts` |
| Rent a car (`packages/shared/src/pricing.ts`) | Limited plan "140 km/day, ₹12/km beyond limit" | No odometer capture, no post-trip settlement, no excess-km billing | Add start/end odometer on the booking and an excess-km settlement step |
| Group vehicles (`apps/web/src/screens/TravelRentalsScreen.tsx`) | "Free cancellation up to 48 hours before trip start. Tolls, parking & state permits paid on actuals" | Same unconditional refund; no line for tolls/permits at quote or settlement | Cancellation tiers as above; add "on actuals" placeholder lines |
| Bus (`packages/shared/src/pricing.ts` lines 26–141; `BusScreens.tsx` lines 321–333) | Pink "Ladies only" seats; five bus types from Volvo 2+2 to Non-AC Seater; amenities including "Live tracking" and "CCTV" | `ladiesChance: 0.08` colours random seats, no API check, so any traveller can book one; every bus renders as a 2+1 two-deck sleeper; amenities are randomly sampled per bus | Replace the random flag with the market rule (lock the adjacent seat after a woman books); render layouts per bus type; seed amenities per bus type |

Three of these (holiday slabs, rental cancellation, group cancellation) collapse into one cancellation-policy engine: a tiered table of hours-before-start to refund percentage, stored per catalog item or per service in `Settings`, applied inside the same `db.batch()` that already writes the refund. That single engine is also the first thing a real bus operator or car owner will demand before listing on a partner portal, and it is the prerequisite for the "free cancellation" paid add-on that redBus and Paytm sell (redBus full refund if cancelled 6–12 hours before departure ([Daily Excelsior](https://www.dailyexcelsior.com/how-redbus-is-redefining-bus-travel-free-cancellation-primo-buses-and-live-tracking/)); Paytm from ₹3 with a 6-hour cutoff ([Paytm](https://paytm.com/offer/travel/travel-free-cancellation/))). Until then, QuikFly's unconditional 100% refund is the most generous policy in the market and also the least realistic.

## Rides: book-now is table stakes; scheduling, women-only and fairness rules are the gap

QuikFly's ride loop (quote → book → driver → OTP → drawn-map tracking → rating, plus a 24-hour public share link, three emergency contacts, auto-share for night rides and admin-tunable ×1.2 surge windows) matches the shape of every competitor's core product (`apps/api/README.md`; `docs/demo-script.md`). What it lacks is the variety layered on top since 2024. **Uber Reserve lets riders book 30 minutes to 90 days ahead** and grew nearly 200% year-on-year in 2024 ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/uber-expands-reserve-option-to-6-more-cities-in-india-news-281431)); **Uber Intercity reached 3,000+ routes by July 2025** with multi-stop, Reserve booking and live tracking, and piloted Motorhomes in four metros ([Uber newsroom](https://www.uber.com/en-IN/newsroom/uber-intercity-expands-to-3000-routes-across-india); [Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/uber-expands-intercity-motorhomes-pilot-to-mumbai-bangalore-and-pune-82289.htm)). inDrive runs rider-set fares with live driver counter-offers and markets "no surge" ([The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/05/20/dcm80-indrive.html); [Mobility Outlook](https://mobilityoutlook.com/news/indrive-challenges-ride-hailing-norms-with-fair-price-model-in-india)). Ola added a non-AC budget tier nationwide ([Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/NEWS/ola-launches-non-ac-ride-category-across-india-130012)), and Namma Yatri's cabs take "special requests" for pets, disability-friendly rides and transport hubs, while its Namma Transit chains metro with first- and last-mile autos in one app ([Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/technology/apps/namma-yatri-launches-zero-commission-cab-service-in-bengaluru-124041601064_1.html); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/07/namma-yatri-launches-namma-transit-public-commute-mobility-bengaluru)). QuikFly has no scheduling, no multi-stop, no outstation cab category, no hourly with-driver rental inside rides, no budget tier and no special requests; backlog item B5 (ride scheduling) is the single most common competitor feature it misses, and all of these are free-tier work on the existing quote engine.

Safety is where the bar rose fastest. **Uber India's November 2024 package added encrypted in-trip audio recording, women-rider preference (female drivers matched only with female riders), SOS integration with state police, and "Safety Preferences" that auto-arm RideCheck, Share My Trip and recording by time or place**; Uber says 95% of its female riders cite safety as their top reason for choosing it ([Uber newsroom](https://www.uber.com/en-IN/newsroom/uber-introduces-new-features-to-elevate-safety)). Uber Moto Women launched women-only bike rides in Bengaluru with 300+ women drivers ([Uber newsroom](https://www.uber.com/en-IN/newsroom/uber-moto-women-women-only-bike-rides-in-bengaluru)), and **Rapido Bike Pink now runs women-captain, women-passenger service in Chennai and Hyderabad** with no fixed login hours ([Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/rapido-pink-bike-launches-in-hyderabad-offering-women-only-rides); [Indian Startup News](https://indianstartupnews.com/news/rapido-launches-bike-pink-in-chennai-for-women-bike-captains)). Rapido bundles Acko insurance and a helmet for every pillion ([Candid.Technology](https://candid.technology/rapido-faq/)); Uber added AI helmet-selfie checks for Moto drivers ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/india/uber-introduces-ai-powered-helmet-selfies-women-rider-preference-and-safety-kits-for-moto-drivers-across-india)); Ola's Guardian flags prolonged stops and route deviations ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/ola-introduces-ai-enabled-safety-feature-guardian-in-16-indian-cities)). QuikFly's SOS sheet, share link and auto-share toggle are structurally equivalent to Share My Trip and a narrow Safety Preferences, but the "alert emergency contacts" step sends nothing (honestly labelled "demo: no SMS sent"), and there is no women-only option (backlog B8), no anomaly detection, no number masking, no insurance line and no rider-chosen PIN. Women-only matching, a generalised Safety Preferences toggle set and a "ride protection ₹X" quote line labelled as demo are free; masking, recording, police hand-off and real alerts need telephony, storage and messaging providers.

Regulation adds a third list. **MVAG 2025 (1 July 2025) caps surge at 2× base fare, sets a cancellation penalty of 10% capped at ₹100 for either side cancelling "without a valid reason" (apps must list acceptable reasons), requires regional-language apps, AIS-140 GPS and panic buttons, 24×7 control rooms and police-verified drivers** ([Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/govt-revises-ride-hailing-rules-2x-surge-cap-cancellation-fee-and-push-for-bike-taxis-9455586); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/cab-aggregators-surge-pricing-rapido-uber-ola-125070200228_1.html)). Maharashtra's draft tightens surge to 1.5× and demands Marathi, Hindi and English with accessibility features ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/maharashtra-govt-issues-draft-rules-to-regulate-ride-hailing-platforms); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/maharashtra-makes-knowing-marathi-mandatory-for-app-based-drivers/)); Delhi permits bike taxis only as EVs ([Energetica India](https://www.energetica-india.net/news/delhi-government-mandates-evs-for-aggregators-in-groundbreaking-scheme)); Karnataka's bike-taxi ban was quashed by the High Court in January 2026 but state policy is still pending ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/karnataka-high-court-lifts-bike-taxi-ban-quashes-siddaramaiah-govt-decision-512638-2026-01-23)). Because QuikFly's fares and surge are already runtime settings with an audit log, a `surgeCap` (default 2.0), a cancellation-reason picker with `cancellationFeePct`/`cancellationFeeCap` (10%/₹100) and per-state availability flags are configuration work, not architecture. Tamil and Hindi string tables are likewise free and will become a compliance item the moment a state adopts the rules; Namma Yatri already ships in five languages ([mwm.ai listing](https://mwm.ai/apps/namma-yatri-ride-booking-app/1637429831)).

Loyalty and economics round out the ride picture. 2025 was the zero-commission year (Ola ₹67/day driver subscription nationwide, Uber autos, Namma Yatri always, Bharat Taxi's cooperative with 51,000+ Delhi drivers at pilot) and it is marketed to riders as "fair fare" ([The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/06/17/dcm77-biz-ola-commission.html); [Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/business/3717015-bharat-taxi-begins-pilot-operation-in-delhi-with-over-51k-registered-drivers-on-mobile-app)). **Uber One costs ₹149/month and returns up to 10% as credits on rides, capped at ₹150 per trip, expiring in 60 days** ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/uber-one-subscription-plan-introduced-in-india-with-discounts-and-perks-check-details-455356-2024-11-27)); Ola Coins earn and redeem across rides, food and courier ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/ola-cabs-rebranded-to-ola-consumer-ceo-aggarwal-launches-loyalty-program-124081500603_1.html)); Rapido's 2019 Power Pass waived surge for commuters ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2019/07/bike-taxi-app-rapido-power-pass-subscription-programme)). QuikFly's wallet, ₹500 welcome credit, ₹100 two-sided referral and rule-based coupons cover the basics; an earn-back credits ledger and a surge-waiving pass are pure logic on `wallet_transactions` and `offers`. The cautionary case is BluSmart, which suspended bookings on 17 April 2025 and left riders with stranded wallet money ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BluSmart); [Boom Live](https://www.boomlive.in/web-stories/news/blusmart-closure-leaves-users-stuck-with-non-refundable-wallet-money-2649)), a reminder to keep promotional credits clearly labelled and non-withdrawable.

## Bus: a star rating no longer signals trust; guarantees and women-first seating do

QuikFly's bus flow (route search, 7-day date strip, filters for departure slot, AC/sleeper, price, rating and operator, a two-deck seat map with per-seat pricing and boarding/dropping points) matches the redBus baseline of several years ago (`apps/web/README.md`; `BusScreens.tsx` lines 95–204). The 2025–26 differentiators are elsewhere. **redBus's revMax engine reprices fares more than 15,000 times a day per service and prices seat categories like airline tiers; GPS tracking covers over 90% of listed buses with wake-up alarms 20 minutes before arrival** ([Autocar Professional](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/the-algorithm-that-adjusts-bus-prices-15000-times-a-day-129061)). AbhiBus tags buses "Brand New" (under 1 year) and "New" (under 2), shows 360° interior photos, and in June 2026 launched roadside assistance that **puts a replacement cab at a broken-down bus within 45 minutes at no cost** ([Autocar Professional](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/abhibus-introduces-filter-to-help-travelers-book-newer-buses-127777); [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/abhibus-launches-industry-first-roadside-assistance-for-bus-breakdowns-on-highways/)). Abhi Assured pays 10–150% compensation for service failures ([ThePrint/ANI](https://theprint.in/ani-press-releases/popular-bus-booking-apps-in-india/2977374/)); **Zingbus refunds 50% of the fare when a bus is more than 45 minutes late** and includes travel insurance on every ticket ([Zingbus](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/is-zingbus-safe-safety-features-reviews/)); redBus Primo curates operators on quality standards and reviews ([Daily Excelsior](https://www.dailyexcelsior.com/how-redbus-is-redefining-bus-travel-free-cancellation-primo-buses-and-live-tracking/)). IntrCity's pickup-guide videos filmed at landmarks cut boarding complaints 85%, and its Smart Switch lets travellers change boarding point, date, time, bus or route up to two hours before departure, cutting cancellations 30% ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/lifestyle/travel/story/flexible-booking-tools-pickup-guides-set-new-benchmark-for-this-intrcity-bus-travel-platform-493422-2025-09-10)).

Women-first design is now a booking driver, not a nicety. **redBus's Pink Report found 47% of women cite "number of women on the bus" as a key factor and 33% rely on ratings by other women; women were 33% of intercity passengers in 2025** ([Coach Builders India](https://coachbuildersindia.com/?p=12216)). **MakeMyTrip automatically limits the adjacent berth to women once a woman books one side of a double berth**, and isolates women-only reviews grouped by punctuality, cleanliness and safety ([Curly Tales](https://curlytales.com/india/travel/makemytrip-introduces-ai-based-safety-assurance-features-to-support-women-travellers/)); NueGo's Pink Seats reserve the seat beside a solo woman for another woman, backed by a 24×7 women's helpline ([Outlook Traveller](https://www.outlooktraveller.com/editors-picks/all-about-nuegos-enhanced-safety-support-for-women-travellers)). QuikFly's random, unenforced pink seats are the inverse of this norm; the market rule (lock the neighbour after a woman books, using the traveller gender QuikFly already collects) is a small change in the seat service and checkout.

Distribution and the frontier matter for positioning. Uber now sells AbhiBus tickets inside its app with real-time seats, 360° photos and the Assured opt-in ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=551393)); Rapido added bus, train, flight and hotel booking via Goibibo, redBus and ConfirmTkt in October 2025 ([Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/business/companies/rapido-launches-travel-section-now-flight-bus-and-train-bookings-possible-on-app-goibibo-redbus-and-confirmtkt-3765355)); Zingbus sells shared electric cabs to the door exclusively with a bus ticket ([Zingbus](https://www.zingbus.com/blog/?p=299)); redBus joined ONDC for metro and auto first/last mile in four metros ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/redbus-taps-ondc-to-offer-metro-auto-rickshaw-bookings/)). **ONDC now moves 375K+ metro and bus tickets a day across 35+ apps**, though intercity remains marketplace-driven ([ONDC](https://ondc.org/pages/about-us.html)). redBus demoed "Ray", a Gemini-based conversational booking assistant, in July 2026, and its support chatbot resolves up to 90% of first contacts ([Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-trends-future/redbus-builds-multilingual-ai-copilots-for-bus-operators-travellers/)). NueGo runs electric intercity in 150+ cities ([Autocar Professional](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/nuego-expands-electric-intercity-bus-network-with-seven-new-routes-133973)). The bundle Zingbus and redBus assemble through partners is QuikFly's native shape: an "add a bike taxi or cab to your boarding point" upsell at bus checkout is unique to QuikFly's position and costs nothing with simulated rides.

Because QuikFly's timetable is generated from routes rather than operator inventory (`docs/demo-script.md`), real GPS, photos and reviews stay gated on an operator feed. What is free and honest now: the cancellation-tier engine plus a "free cancellation" add-on quote line; Smart-Switch-style reschedule (backlog B6) and partial seat cancellation (QuikFly currently cancels the whole booking); the women-first seat rule; per-type seat layouts; an m-ticket screen with PNR and QR plus a public trip page reusing the ride share-token pattern; a fare-aware date strip with a demand multiplier like the ride surge; and a settings-driven "QuikFly Assured" on-time credit to the wallet, which is credible only because the wallet is real and atomic. SMS/WhatsApp ticket delivery, live tracking, insurance and the LLM assistant wait for sign-off.

## Holidays: QuikFly sells a format no leading competitor uses

The holiday module is the furthest from market. QuikFly offers a fixed private package for any date with one static itinerary, per-adult pricing with a child percentage and a staff-typed rating (`types.ts` lines 220–240; `pricing.ts` lines 162–169). The Indian market sells two other shapes. Group-tour operators sell dated departures: **Veena World lists each departure with seats left and price, flags "Dates Filling Fast", "sold out" or joining/leaving-only, states that prices rise as seats fill, and guarantees a room partner for solo travellers on women's and seniors' tours**, with 350+ tour managers each having a public profile ([Veena World](https://www.veenaworld.com/package/amsterdam-brussels-bruges-tour-package-eubr); [Veena World FAQ](https://www.veenaworld.com/faq); [Seniors Special](https://www.veenaworld.com/campaign/seniors-special-tours)). Kesari browses by month with date counts, shows city pages like "Mumbai: 183 tours, 1,046 departures", and offers "Book Online / Request Callback / Get Itinerary" on every tour ([Kesari](https://www.kesari.in/)). Customisers sell editable itineraries: Pickyourtrail's "fully personalised, editable day-by-day plan" with optional flights, visa assistance tables and no-cost EMI ([Pickyourtrail](https://pickyourtrail.com/packages)); Yatra lets you swap hotels ([Yatra](https://packages.yatra.com/holidays)); **Cleartrip entered holidays in September 2026 with expert-built, traveller-editable itineraries, an AI clash resolver and a "Price Beat" promise of 5% below any rival quote**, in a holidays segment Skift sizes at about $20 billion and more than 90% fragmented ([Skift](https://skift.com/?p=841419)).

Financing and human help surround every competitor's checkout. **EaseMyTrip lets travellers lock a package for ₹999 and pay later** ([EaseMyTrip](https://www.easemytrip.com/holidays/book-at-999-tours-packages/)); **SOTC offers no-cost EMI over 3–24 months** ([SOTC](https://www.sotc.in/emi-holidays)); Kesari and Veena World print "EMI from ₹X/month" on cards, and Veena World runs a "Holiday Cart" for balance payments ([Kesari](https://www.kesari.in/); [Veena World](https://www.veenaworld.com/package/japan-korea-taiwan-tour-package-asjt)). Thrillophilia puts "Request callback" on every card and shows 4.6 from 60,799 reviews with a "With media" photo-review filter ([Thrillophilia](https://www.thrillophilia.com/tags/group-tours)); Kesari and Veena World publish WhatsApp chat numbers and purpose-specific toll-free lines ([Kesari](https://www.kesari.in/)). AI assistants are the 2025–26 headline: **MakeMyTrip's Myra 2.0 (May 2026) completes bookings by voice and auto-fills traveller details from a passport upload, handling over 3 million conversations a quarter with 45%+ from tier-2 and smaller cities** ([India TV](https://www.indiatvnews.com/technology/news/makemytrip-myra-2-0-ai-asssistant-to-help-completing-travel-bookings-through-voice-conversations-2026-05-14-1041087)); Yatra's DIYA handles planning through cancellations in 100+ languages ([TravelTrendsToday](https://www.traveltrendstoday.in/yatra-launches-diya-for-end-to-end-planning-booking-multilingual-support-with-ai)); EaseMyTrip became bookable inside the ChatGPT app in April 2026 ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/openais-chatgpt-app-integrates-easemytrip-what-it-means-for-users-523894-2026-04-03)).

Demand has also shifted away from QuikFly's theme list (beach, hills, international, weekend, heritage). **Pilgrimage accounts for 48% of domestic overnight trips** per the MoSPI survey ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/economy/story/domestic-tourism-nearly-1-in-2-overnight-trips-in-india-are-for-pilgrimage-religious-purposes-557348-2026-09-23)); Char Dham package bookings rose 73% in January–August 2025 and Ayodhya 40% ([Curly Tales](https://curlytales.com/india/travel/rise-in-char-dham-bookings-surge-noted-in-ayodhya-as-spiritual-tourism-continues-to-boom-in-india/amp/)); solo women travellers on IntrCity grew 135% from 2023 to 2025 ([Curly Tales](https://curlytales.com/india/travel/india-sees-rise-in-solo-women-travellers-since-says-report/)); women-only and seniors' departures are established categories at Veena World, Kesari ("My Fair Lady", "Second Innings") and EaseMyTrip ([Veena World](https://veenaworld.com/campaign/womens-special-tours); [Kesari](https://kesari.in/Speciality-Tours/My-Fair-Lady-Tours); [EaseMyTrip](https://www.easemytrip.com/holidays/women-special-tours-packages/)). Adding spiritual, women-only and seniors themes is a one-line type change plus seed data; audience-restricted departures need the departures model.

The free-tier order that follows is: first make the detail page truthful (slab refunds, drop or build the 25% block and the callback and insurance lines); second, departures with dates, seats and departure-city/month filters, which the quote must accept; third, a `POST /enquiries` callback/quote queue in admin plus a `wa.me` click-to-chat link (the WhatsApp Business API is paid); fourth, verified photo reviews tied to completed bookings (R2's free tier covers photo storage); fifth, a tour-manager field and a printable itinerary voucher built like the existing GST invoice. EMI/BNPL, real part-payment, hotel and flight supplier customisation, visa and insurance partners, multi-currency and a production LLM planner stay post-sign-off; a constrained "demo" planner ranking existing packages on Workers AI's free allocation is possible if labelled. The positioning prize is that no OTA owns ground transport: a Char Dham or weekend circuit sold as QuikFly cab pickup, QuikFly bus or tempo traveller, package at destination, is a door-to-door story Cleartrip and MakeMyTrip buy from third parties.

## Rentals and group hire: the post-booking lifecycle is missing

QuikFly's self-drive flow (two km plans, two protection tiers, doorstep delivery, flat refundable deposit, admin-reviewed licence) and its chauffeur mode (hourly plus driver-per-day) are a credible instant-booking front end, and the group-vehicle estimator with a side-by-side compare of up to three vehicles has no documented equal (`TravelRentalsScreen.tsx`; `settings.ts`). What is absent is everything after payment. **Zoomcar, now a pure peer-to-peer marketplace, requires Aadhaar + licence + live-selfie KYC, opens a photo pickup checklist 30 minutes before start that must be completed or Trip Protection is void, lets guests change date, time, duration, vehicle and delivery mode until 2 hours before start, prices extensions with proportional protection, charges up to 2× the hourly fee for late return, bills excess km from odometer readings, releases deposits within 24 hours with itemised deductions and a 7-day dispute window, and publishes a penalty schedule (₹10,000 lost key, ₹2,500 for 125 km/h)** ([Zoomcar FAQs](https://www.zoomcar.com/faq); [Zoomcar fee schedule](https://policy.zoomcar.com/support/solutions/articles/16000146256-here-is-a-comprehensive-list-of-fees-and-penalties-for-certain-scenarios-after-you-have-created-your-)). Its cancellation ladder is no refund within 6 hours, the lower of 50% or ₹4,000 between 6 hours and 15 days, and 100% beyond. In November 2025 Zoomcar backed Trip Protection with a Universal Sompo contractual liability policy ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/zoomcar-introduces-trip-protection-supported-by-universal-sompos-contractual-liability-policy-302626488.html)), and in October 2026 added Bureau real-time fraud verification ([Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/bureau-partners-with-zoomcar-for-real-time-user-verification-135116)). Its CY2025 host report shows **Home Delivery hosts earning about 1.9× others and repeat renters supplying 56.5% of earnings** ([Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/665267/zoomcar-reports-cy-2025-host-payouts-of-12-8m-with-56-5-of-earnings-from-repeat-renters)), evidence that QuikFly's doorstep option and a "book again" prompt are the right bets. Fleet operators run fixed km packages (MyChoize 120/300/unlimited, full-tank fuel rule, ₹6,000 deposit in most cities) and DigiLocker licences (Revv) ([MyChoize FAQ](https://www.mychoize.com/faq); [Revv FAQ](https://www.revv.co.in/faq/hyderabad)); Myles computes extra km at ₹11–14/km at drop-off and deducts cancellation of ₹200 or 50% ([Myles FAQ](https://mylescars.com/faqs)).

Chauffeur outstation is a product QuikFly does not have at all. Savaari (majority-owned by MakeMyTrip since January 2024) sells one-way as all-inclusive and round trips as per-km with night allowance 10 PM–6 AM and tolls on actuals, and its **driver-language preference was used on 13% of trips and lifted NPS 20% on those trips** ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/makemytrip-acquires-majority-stake-in-savaari-to-enter-intercity-cab-market); [Savaari](https://www.savaari.com/car-rental/taxi-in-hyderabad); [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/savaari-car-rentals-enhances-intercity-travel-with-driver-language-preference-feature-123640)). Gozo quotes a booking-specific package with included km and extra-km rate, requires extra stops to be approved in the itinerary, shares driver details on a live link, and accepts advance plus cash balance to the driver ([Gozo FAQ](https://www.gozocabs.com/faq)); Bharat Taxi lists Local, One Way, Round Trip and Road Trip with night allowance after 10 pm and free cancellation beyond 24 hours ([Bharat Taxi](https://www.bharattaxi.com/)). QuikFly's group engine (per-km × max(actual, min km/day) + bata) already has the right shape and can be reused as an outstation cab quote type with night-allowance and "on actuals" lines.

Group hire in India is a request-for-quote market: redBus Bus Hire returns operator quotes by email with vehicle registration and photos, historically with 50% advance ([redBus blog](https://www.redbus.in/blog/tempo-traveller-on-rent-redbus-hire/); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2017/01/223-redbus-bus-car-hiring/)); TransRentals compares 500+ operators on price, inclusions and ratings with a 15-minute callback, prices one-way 10–15% above round trip per km and non-AC 15–20% cheaper, and warns of 3–4 week lead times for Char Dham ([TransRentals](https://transrentals.in/bus-rental)). QuikFly's instant estimate from a house fleet is simpler for the customer; the moment third-party operators list (backlog partner portals) it will need a quote/accept flow, an AC/non-AC toggle, one-way versus round-trip rates and an advance/balance model. BlaBlaCar's surge (13.5 million Indian trips to September 2025, +42% in H1 2026 on fuel prices) hints at a "fill the tempo" seat-sharing adjacent ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/25/india-the-market-blablacar-once-walked-away-from-is-now-its-biggest); [Outlook Traveller](https://www.outlooktraveller.com/News/how-carpooling-is-becoming-an-integral-part-of-indias-domestic-travel-network)).

Free-tier moves, in order: odometer and fuel-level capture with excess-km settlement at drop-off; photo checklists stored as booking attachments (R2 free tier) with protection gated on completion; Manage Booking and extension (backlog B7) with a late-return multiplier; API-enforced cancellation tiers; co-driver registration; a deposit lifecycle with release and itemised deductions recorded as wallet transactions; an outstation chauffeur quote type; weekly/monthly tiers with a km/day allowance; and a request-a-quote mode for group hire with staff-entered operator quotes in admin. Document-image and selfie KYC (Onfido/Bureau-class vendors), DigiLocker, keyless and GPS hardware, card or UPI deposit holds, insurer-backed protection, FASTag and challan reconciliation and 24×7 roadside assistance contracts are paid and should be labelled "after sign-off".

## Platform: push is free today, WhatsApp costs ₹0.13, compliance has a 2027 deadline

Cross-cutting, QuikFly covers more ground than its six tiles suggest (one wallet and ledger, server-side quotes, 27 runtime settings and 12 catalog kinds behind an audited three-role admin, GST invoices, referral, saved places, travellers and contacts, licence KYC queue, deletion with 30-day grace, light/dark tokens on web and Android) but it has no engagement rail at all: no push, no WhatsApp, no chat, no tickets, no assistant. The leaders converge on three things QuikFly lacks. First, paid membership with cashback: Uber One at ₹149/month returns up to 10% in credits capped at ₹150 per trip, with priority support and top-rated drivers as perks, and now extends to linked teens with a shared family hub ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/uber-one-subscription-plan-introduced-in-india-with-discounts-and-perks-check-details-455356-2024-11-27); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/11/uber-expands-teens-family-features-teens-link-accounts)). Second, a points currency that works across every service: GrabCoins (renamed November 2025, earned on third-party partner apps too), Tata's NeuCoin at ₹1, GoPay Coins ([Grab](https://www.grab.com/sg/press/others/grab-launches-third-party-partner-apps-within-grab-app-offering-more-everyday-services-for-everyday-needs/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2274308)). Third, family and business profiles: **Grab's Family Account lets one payer cover up to nine members, see their rides live and auto-arm audio protection** ([Grab](https://www.grab.com/inside-grab/stories/grab-family-account-feature-launch/)); Uber for Teens in India uses destination-locked trips and high-rated drivers only, with a seniors profile added ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/uber-pilots-accounts-for-teenagers-in-india)); MakeMyTrip's myBiz serves 75,000+ organisations and passed $1 billion in corporate bookings in 2025 ([myBiz](https://mybiz.makemytrip.com/corporate/business-travel-for-SMEs.html); [Asian Hospitality](https://www.asianhospitality.com/makemytrip-1b-corporate-travel-bookings/)). All three are data-model work on tables QuikFly already has: a credits ledger is new `wallet_transactions` kinds plus a percentage-and-cap setting; a family profile reuses the share link and emergency contacts; a business profile is a GSTIN per profile and a monthly consolidated invoice.

The cost picture for the paid rails is better than the roadmap assumes. **Firebase Cloud Messaging is listed as no-cost with no documented message limits** ([Firebase pricing](https://firebase.google.com/pricing)), so push for booking status, refunds and licence decisions is a ₹0 addition on both the Flutter app and the static site. **Truecaller's SDK is free with no usage limits** and verifies most Indian Android users without an SMS ([Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/reources/resource-partners/application-based-services/Truecaller_New.html)), leaving SMS OTP at roughly ₹0.12–0.30 per message plus 18% GST and DLT registration as the fallback ([BonVoice](https://bonvoice.com/insights/best-otp-sms-service-india-2026/)). **WhatsApp utility messages cost about ₹0.13 each in India since Meta's July 2025 per-message pricing**, plus a BSP markup (Gupshup USD 0.001 per message, no setup fee) ([MyOperator](https://support.myoperator.com/portal/en/kb/articles/whatsapp-has-shifted-to-per-message-pricing-effective-july-1-2025-based-on-message-categories-and-your-recipient-s-country-in-india-there-are-three-paid-categories); [Gupshup](https://www.gupshup.ai/isv-partners/whatsapp-api/pricing)), so "booking confirmed, driver assigned, refund credited" costs a few rupees per booking but needs a verified Meta Business account. **Razorpay charges 2% + GST with ₹0 setup, AMC or refund fees** ([Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/pricing/)), the lowest-commitment payment entry; Cashfree's reported ₹4,999 AMC makes it worse at low volume ([SureCart](https://surecart.com/blog/razorpay-vs-cashfree-vs-payu-wordpress-india/)). **Google Maps Platform gives Indian developers up to $6,800/month of free usage with the Maps SDK unlimited free** since March 2025 ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/map-wars-google-offers-free-access-of-up-to-6800-to-indian-developers)), which means a live map with Places autocomplete at demo volumes is effectively ₹0 if `CityMap.tsx` keeps its props and swaps the renderer; Ola Maps advertised 5 million free calls per month at launch, though the current figure is disputed ([Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/companies/start-ups/krutrim-takes-on-google-unveils-new-pricing-future-roadmap-for-ola-maps-124071801308_1.html)). Store presence is $25 one-time for Play (new personal accounts need 12 testers for 14 days) and $99/year for Apple ([TMS Outsource](https://tms-outsource.com/blog/posts/google-play-developer-account-requirements/); [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-play-app-testing-requirement-3510580)), and Google's developer verification for sideloaded apps starts in four countries in September 2026 with a global rollout planned for 2027 ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/android-sideloading-changes-timeline-3679204/)), so the $25 account is worth paying before sign-off as a hedge for the APK-on-GitHub distribution.

Compliance is the one platform item with a hard date. **The DPDP Rules 2025 were notified on 14 November 2025; the substantive duties (plain-language consent notices listing data, purpose and how to withdraw; breach notice to affected users "without delay"; erasure with 48 hours' notice; a published DPO or authorised-person contact; verifiable parental consent) take effect after 18 months, around May 2027** ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/11/digital-personal-data-protection-dpdp-rules-2025-rollout-over-18-months)). QuikFly's 30-day deletion grace already exceeds the 48-hour notice; the rest is a consent screen, a privacy page, a data-export endpoint and a breach template. On money, closed-system prepaid instruments where the issuer is the only redeemer sit outside RBI PPI authorisation, and the April 2026 draft keeps that exemption while explicitly excluding marketplaces ([Vinod Kothari](https://vinodkothari.com/2026/04/rbis-draft-ppi-norms-stricter-cash-rules-simplified-categories-no-cross-border-payments-and-more/); [Mondaq](https://webiis10.mondaq.com/india/financial-services/1797746/decoding-the-key-changes-proposed-under-the-simplified-master-direction-on-prepaid-payment-instruments-issued-by-the-rbi)), so QuikFly's wallet is fine while QuikFly is the sole seller but becomes a question the day partner operators are paid from it. GST e-invoicing applies only above ₹5 crore turnover ([Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/e-invoicing-limit-india/)).

Multi-modal is the strategic platform gap. Namma Transit chains metro with first/last-mile autos and tells the rider which gate and platform to use ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/07/namma-yatri-launches-namma-transit-public-commute-mobility-bengaluru)); Yandex Go's Hub folds all public transport and passes into one section ([www1.ru](https://www1.ru/en/news/2025/06/08/vse-dorogi-v-xab-iandeks-go-obieedinil-ves-transport-v-odnom-servise.html)); Uber's April 2026 Go-Get added hotels behind one search bar ([Axios](https://axios.com/2026/04/29/uber-app-hotels-expansion)). QuikFly sells six verticals with no object spanning them; a "Trip" container that groups bookings into one timeline with one combined invoice and a "book the first mile" prompt on a confirmed bus or holiday is free to build and depends only on ride scheduling (B5). The cautionary signals argue for restraint in the other direction: Gojek shut its tiered GoClub loyalty in February 2024 ([Bisnis.com](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20240224/266/1743753/gojek-tutup-layanan-loyalitas-pelanggan-goclub-per-26-februari)), Ola's Kruti assistant went offline by April 2026 ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/bhavish-aggarwals-ai-bet-hits-another-snag-as-kruti-goes-offline)), PhonePe dropped Pincode's consumer app for B2B tools in December 2025 ([PhonePe](https://www.phonepe.com/press/pincode-to-focus-exclusively-on-b2b-business-solutions-for-merchants/)), and Paytm is retreating from super-app framing ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=513349)). Simple credits beat tiered gamification, a scripted assistant beats a general one, and partner portals beat new consumer verticals.

## Where QuikFly is already ahead or distinctive

QuikFly's advantages are structural and should be named as such in the client pitch, because no competitor studied makes the same combination of claims in a single app. **One real wallet across six travel modes with every debit or credit written atomically alongside its ledger row** (AGENTS.md Rule 6) is the hard part of any cross-service loyalty scheme, and it already exists; competitors' wallets are single-vertical (Ola Money) and BluSmart's collapse showed the trust stakes. **Instant, atomic wallet refunds on customer or staff cancellation** (`bookings.ts` lines 114–129; demonstrated live in `docs/demo-script.md` step 5) beat the market's 6–12 hour cutoffs and 7–10 working-day bank refunds (Zoomcar) ([Zoomcar FAQs](https://www.zoomcar.com/faq)) and the refund disputes that dog Thrillophilia and EaseMyTrip ([Voxya](https://voxya.com/consumer-complaints/refund-not-provided-by-thrillophilia-/244468)); once the slab engine exists, the speed stays and the policy becomes honest. **The API sets every price via single-use 30-minute quotes**, so web and app cannot drift and coupons are rejected with reasons server-side (AGENTS.md Rule 5; `apps/api/README.md`). **Runtime-tunable fares, surge windows, GST rates and bonuses with a before/after audit log, reaching web and app in 30 seconds without a deploy**, map directly onto MVAG's state-notified base fares and surge caps ([Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/govt-revises-ride-hailing-rules-2x-surge-cap-cancellation-fee-and-push-for-bike-taxis-9455586)); competitors' terms differ by city and page with visible contradictions (MyChoize ₹6,000 vs ₹3,000 vs zero deposit) ([MyChoize FAQ](https://www.mychoize.com/faq)).

Several smaller things are also distinctive. **GST invoices with GSTIN captured on the profile and a CGST/SGST split** were not found as a highlighted feature in any competitor source and were the original pitch of MakeMyTrip's myBiz to SMEs ([Business Insider India](https://www.businessinsider.in/i-tried-the-makemytrip-my-business-facility-which-makes-gst-claim-easier-for-smes/articleshow/60782245.cms)). The **instant per-km group-vehicle estimate with a side-by-side compare of up to three vehicles** has no documented equal; redBus Hire and TransRentals return quotes by email or callback ([redBus blog](https://www.redbus.in/blog/tempo-traveller-on-rent-redbus-hire/); [TransRentals](https://transrentals.in/bus-rental)). **Self-drive and chauffeur in one listing with one quote engine** contrasts with Revv and Zoomcar being self-drive only ([Revv](https://www.revv.co.in/car-rental/coimbatore); [Zoomcar FAQs](https://www.zoomcar.com/faq)). The licence review queue with reject reasons, resubmission and a status that updates on the phone in 5–7 seconds is the demo's strongest moment (`docs/demo-script.md` step 7). The public ride-share link needs no sign-in and expires in 24 hours, the SOS sheet dials 112 and a configurable safety line, and auto-share for night rides is a lightweight Safety Preferences ([Uber newsroom](https://www.uber.com/en-IN/newsroom/uber-introduces-new-features-to-elevate-safety)). The 30-day deletion grace already exceeds DPDP's 48-hour notice. Honest labelling of every simulation ("demo: no SMS sent") compares well with competitor tracking marketed as live but failing in reviews ([Trustpilot](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/redbus.in?page=3)). Finally, light/dark tokens, reduced-motion support, 390 px checks on every screen and web/Android parity on one API, all at zero running cost on Workers + D1 free tiers, is a delivery posture most startups at this stage do not have. The positioning line that falls out is "one wallet, one price engine, one back office across six travel modes", with "door-to-door holidays on our own fleet" as the story no OTA can tell.

## Conclusion

The research reframes QuikFly's position: it is not behind on infrastructure, where its single API, atomic wallet and audited settings are ahead of players several funding rounds larger, but behind on the behaviours that infrastructure should drive. The most striking finding is that QuikFly's own UI has already written the spec for its next sprint (slab refunds, block payments, night charges, km caps, ladies seats) and the API simply never read it; closing that gap converts the product's most generous-looking policy into its most credible one, and the same cancellation engine then becomes the gate for every flexibility feature the market now sells. The second shift in understanding is that the "free tier" boundary has moved: FCM push, Truecaller login and Google Maps' $6,800/month allowance mean three of the five rails the roadmap parks behind sign-off can ship now at ₹0 or $25, leaving only payments, messaging and insurance as true client-funded items.

Looking forward, two external clocks set the order of work. MVAG 2025 and the DPDP Rules give QuikFly a compliance shape (2× surge cap, 10%/₹100 cancellation with listed reasons, regional languages, consent notices and a DPO contact by about May 2027) that competitors must also meet, so building it into `Settings` and `rules.ts` now turns regulation into a feature list rather than a retrofit. And the market's own corrections (Gojek closing tiered loyalty, Ola's assistant going offline, PhonePe retreating to B2B, BluSmart's stranded wallets) argue that QuikFly's small team should resist copying the flashiest competitor moves and instead press the one claim nobody else can make: one wallet, one price engine and one back office across six modes of Indian travel, with the ground fleet to deliver a holiday door to door.
